# stepped-editorial

## Look

Editorial spreads with offset plates, serif editorial type and yellow-marker evidence; boxless captions.

## Best for

Opinions, case studies and stories with a magazine feel.

## Layouts and footage

Uses the shared `split` and `takeover` layouts. [MOTION-CHOICES.md](../MOTION-CHOICES.md) records each family's layout support and [PERFORMANCE.md](../PERFORMANCE.md) records presenter picture-in-picture.

## Palette and brand

- **Brand colours:** not applied.

## Recipe

**Direction:** develop a thesis through details

- **Decision:** Choose it for an opinion, case study or story that starts with a thesis and advances through supporting details. Each new detail should change what the viewer understands.
- **Composition/material:** Build an editorial spread with offset plates, serif hierarchy and yellow-marker evidence. Treat the marked excerpt as a focal detail, not a highlighter applied to every line.
- **Timing/action:** Set the thesis, bring in an evidence/detail plate, guide attention to the relevant fragment, then resolve its implication. Hold the marked text before shifting the spread's hierarchy.
- **Transition/sound:** Carry the selected excerpt, plate edge or marker path into the next idea. A page/plate movement can connect paragraphs; a constant camera drift can reduce reading clarity. Effects follow [SOUND.md](../SOUND.md).
- **Application/review:** Combine supported proof/comparison/process scenes or a project spread. Check the reading order and whether the evidence actually supports the thesis. Avoid several simultaneous plates competing with the source captions.

## Style-owned scenes

- Treat the marked excerpt as a focal detail, not a highlighter applied to every line.
