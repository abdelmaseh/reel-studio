# campaign-tickets

## Look

Warm gold/coral tickets, perforated cards and a campaign canvas.

## Best for

Real offers, invitations and viewer questions.

## Layouts and footage

Uses the shared `split` and `takeover` layouts. [MOTION-CHOICES.md](../MOTION-CHOICES.md) records each family's layout support and [PERFORMANCE.md](../PERFORMANCE.md) records presenter picture-in-picture.

## Palette and brand

- **Brand colours:** not applied; the style keeps its own palette.

## Recipe

**Direction:** explain the invitation and its conditions

- **Decision:** Choose it for a real offer, invitation or viewer question. Establish the action the viewer can take and the terms they need before designing urgency.
- **Composition/material:** Use warm tickets, coral stubs and perforated surfaces. Let the offer/action dominate while the conditions remain readable; separate real attributed questions from illustrative examples.
- **Timing/action:** Reveal what is offered, demonstrate the concrete benefit, show conditions, then give the approved action time. A question stack reveals one meaningful question at a time, not an unreadable pile.
- **Transition/sound:** Carry a ticket or stub from invitation to action. A tear/click can accompany that graphic movement; it does not imply a purchase or completed transaction. Effects follow [SOUND.md](../SOUND.md).
- **Application/review:** `ticket-offer` and `comment-stack` supply validated fields. Verify price/value, dates, conditions, attribution and exact approved CTA. Never add a fake countdown, scarcity, discount or testimonial to make the ticket look convincing.

## Style-owned scenes

No scene family is reserved for this style. The beats below are shared families that make it eligible for automatic recommendation.

- **Semantic beats:** `ticket-offer` and `comment-stack`.
- Use the real offer conditions and real comments, or clearly labelled illustrative examples.

## Avoid

- A tear/click can accompany that graphic movement; it does not imply a purchase or completed transaction.
- Never add a fake countdown, scarcity, discount or testimonial to make the ticket look convincing.
