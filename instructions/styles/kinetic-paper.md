# kinetic-paper

## Look

Warm paper, oversized Lalezar headlines and Cairo body copy; black ink slabs, lime edges, perforated tickets and stacked comment cards. Fixed pilot choreography; saved headline fonts take precedence.

## Best for

Explainers, comparisons and tutorials that want a strong, poster-like opening.

## Layouts and footage

Uses the shared `split` and `takeover` layouts. [MOTION-CHOICES.md](../MOTION-CHOICES.md) records each family's layout support and [PERFORMANCE.md](../PERFORMANCE.md) records presenter picture-in-picture.

## Palette and brand

- **Brand colours:** not applied; the style keeps its own palette.

## Recipe

**Direction:** make one statement land

- **Decision:** Choose it for a strong claim, question or contrast that benefits from poster-scale hierarchy. Break the argument into meaningful units rather than animating every spoken word equally.
- **Composition/material:** Let Lalezar dominate the warm paper; lime ticket edges and black ink objects support the headline. Shared ticket-offer and comment-stack use their own pilot drawings.
- **Timing/action:** Keep the approved fixed cues and settled reading holds. Use whole Arabic words; presenter statements respect the supplied head box.
- **Transition/sound:** Prefer the signature `ink-slab` between compatible adjacent takeover scenes. This is a director choice: the code preserves authored transitions and does not propose them automatically. Follow its layout and two-scene contract in [MOTION-CHOICES.md](../MOTION-CHOICES.md); sound follows [SOUND.md](../SOUND.md).
- **Application/review:** Use reviewed titles or a suitable `motion-concept`; literal `kinetic-text` requires real recorded word indices. Check that text motion expresses emphasis and leaves enough reading time, without treating authored titles as subtitles.

- **Semantic beats:** `kinetic-text`, `ticket-offer` and `comment-stack` make this style eligible for automatic recommendation. They remain shared families with the same editable data.
